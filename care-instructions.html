Care Instructions

Hand block-printed cotton rewards a little gentleness — here's how to keep it beautiful for years.

Washing

  • Machine wash cold (30°C or below) on a gentle cycle, separately for the first two washes as natural dyes can release a little colour early on.
  • Use a mild detergent. Avoid bleach or harsh whiteners — they fade block-printed colour fast.
  • Wash with like colours, not with heavily lint-producing fabrics like towels.

Drying

  • Line dry in shade where possible. Direct, prolonged sun exposure can fade natural dyes over time.
  • If using a dryer, choose a low-heat setting and remove promptly to minimise creasing.

Ironing

Iron on a medium setting while slightly damp for the crispest finish. Percale-weave bedsheets in particular soften and smooth out further with each wash.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place. Cotton breathes best when folded loosely rather than compressed — a linen bag or open shelf is better than a sealed plastic bin.

A note on variation

Because every piece is stamped by hand, you may notice slight differences in colour depth or print alignment between pieces, and even across a single sheet. This isn't a flaw — it's the signature of hand block printing, and part of why each piece is genuinely one of a kind.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 July 2026

Ekaika Living ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used. This is a general-purpose policy for a small independent storefront, not a substitute for formal legal advice.

What we collect

  • Information you give us — name, email, phone number, and shipping address, when you contact us, subscribe to our newsletter, or place an order.
  • Local device storage — this site uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your bag and your saved/wishlist items. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

How we use it

We use the information you give us to respond to enquiries, process and ship orders, and — only if you opt in — send occasional newsletter emails. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Who we share it with

We may share order details with delivery and logistics partners solely to fulfil your order. We do not share your information with advertisers.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing ekaikaliving@gmail.com. You can clear your bag and saved items at any time from your browser's site settings, since that data lives only on your device.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage purely to remember your shopping bag and saved items between visits — this is functional, not used for tracking, and never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data will reset your bag and saved items.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at ekaikaliving@gmail.com.
